◌ RECALL FOR SAFARI

Save anything you find.

This adds a local Safari bookmark that captures the current image, product name, visible price, and original link. Nothing is sent to a server.

Save to Recall
  1. In Safari choose View → Show Favorites Bar.
  2. Drag the orange Save to Recall button onto that bar.
  3. Visit a product or image page and click the new bookmark.

If dragging is blocked, create any bookmark, choose Bookmarks → Edit Bookmarks, and replace its address with the copied address.
